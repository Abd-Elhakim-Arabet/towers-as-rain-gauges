Algeria Storm Replay

Two real Algerian floods, replayed over a simulated network of microwave links on real tower positions. Top left: the rain we simulated. The other maps show the rain map the links give back, from all links and from only 3% of them, and where the AI reads too much or too little.

Simulation Storms from NASA satellites, towers from OpenCelliD, link noise from real Italian recordings. It shows the chain working on Algeria. It does not prove accuracy there.
